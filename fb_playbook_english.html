How Facebook Pays Creators in 2026

01

Facebook now has one program called Content Monetization. One approval lets you earn from all your posts.

~$3 billion

Facebook paid creators nearly this much in 2025, its highest ever.

60%

of that money went to Reels. So Reels are your main tool.

What changed?

The old programs (In-Stream Ads, Ads on Reels and the Performance Bonus) are now merged into this one program. Creator Studio is gone. Everything is in your Professional Dashboard.

How to join (it's invite-only)

  1. Open the Facebook app and go to your Professional Dashboard.
  2. Tap Monetization, then Content Monetization.
  3. Fill in the "express interest" form.
  4. Keep posting original content. Facebook sends invites from time to time.

You live in India, but your viewers can be in America. Here's how.

👉 Don't wake up at 5 AM. Use the scheduler in Meta Business Suite.

2️⃣ Write like an American

Use US spelling (color, not colour), dollars ($), miles, °F and US examples. Keep captions, on-screen text and voice in English.

3️⃣ Pick US topics

Open Google Trends, set it to United States. A video on US home prices brings US viewers. A video on Indian mutual funds brings Indian viewers.

4️⃣ Get US followers first

Facebook first shows your post to your followers and similar people. If your early followers are American, every new post leans American.

🚫 Don't use a VPN

Fake locations can trigger security checks and don't change who watches.

📍 Weekly audience check

  1. Go to Professional Dashboard → Insights → Audience → Top countries.
  2. Target: 60%+ viewers from US, UK, Canada and Australia by Day 30.
  3. If India is above 40%, check your last 10 posts: topic, examples and posting time.

The Faceless AI Video Machine

03

Make one 45–60 second Reel in 15–25 minutes. Make 5 at once and it gets close to 10 minutes each.

✍️ Step 1: Write a script people watch till the end

Hook

First 2 sec. Make them curious.

Promise

Say what they'll get at the end.

3–5 points

A surprise every 8–10 sec.

Payoff

Deliver the promise.

Question

A real question to answer.

📋 Copy this prompt into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini:

You are a scriptwriter for a faceless Facebook Reels page in the [NICHE] niche. Audience: adults in the United States, watching on mobile. Write a [45/60/90]-second voiceover script about: [TOPIC]. Rules: - Line 1: a hook under 12 words that creates curiosity. - Line 2: promise a clear payoff that comes at the end. - 3-5 short points. Add a surprise every 8-10 seconds. - Use American English, US examples, dollars and miles. - Facts must be correct. Mark anything unsure with [VERIFY]. - End with the payoff, then one real question viewers can answer from their own life. No "comment YES" lines. - Output 3 columns: VOICEOVER | ON-SCREEN TEXT (max 6 words) | VISUAL IDEA - Give 3 extra hook options at the end.
Always check [VERIFY] lines. Wrong facts can get your post labeled as false, which cuts views and money.

✂️ Step 4: Edit like a pro

📵 CapCut is blocked in India. Use VN Video Editor, Canva, DaVinci Resolve (free) or Premiere Pro.
  1. Put the voice first. Cut every silence longer than a third of a second.
  2. Change the picture every 2–3 seconds: new clip, small zoom-in (105–110%), or a slow pan.
  3. Big captions, 2–4 words at a time, white text with black outline, key word in yellow.
  4. Add a soft "whoosh" on cuts and a rising sound before the big reveal.
  5. Music only from Meta Sound Collection or a licensed library. Keep it quiet (10–15%).
  6. Export at 1080 × 1920, 30 fps.

🏷️ Step 5: Use the AI label

If your AI video or voice looks or sounds real, turn on the AI label when posting. This keeps your page safe. The label alone does not reduce your earnings.

Get More Views the Right Way

04

Big reach comes from real people watching, sharing and commenting. Fake shortcuts get pages punished.

🚫 Don't do this: sharing through fake or extra accounts, posting the same link in dozens of groups, or "comment pods". Facebook treats this as spam. Pages lose reach, lose money or get removed.

✅ The safe way to spread your content

👥
Start your own Group

Make a Facebook Group for your niche and link it to your Page. Post a daily question. Share your best Reel there once.

🤝
Join 5–10 niche groups

Pick active groups that allow sharing. Help people for a week first. Then share only matching posts, max once a week per group.

🔗
Team up with pages

Find 3–5 similar-size pages. Share each other's posts or make posts together.

📲
Post everywhere at once

Post the same Reel to Facebook and Instagram from Meta Business Suite. Share it to your Story.

♻️ Reuse winners: When a Reel does well, make a longer video, a photo post and a text post from it.

Original Content Rules

05

In 2026 Facebook gives more reach and money to original content and less to copied content. Views on original Reels roughly doubled in late 2025.

✅ ORIGINAL
  • Videos you made with your own script, AI voice and visuals
  • Remixes where you add new facts or real analysis
  • Licensed stock clips arranged into your own story
❌ NOT ORIGINAL
  • Re-uploading other people's videos
  • Adding borders, captions or speed changes to someone else's video
  • Joining other people's clips or just reacting
⚠️ Watch out: Many online tips say "add a border, flip it, speed it up". Facebook now names exactly these tricks as unoriginal.

🔍 How Facebook spots copied videos

Facebook's Rights Manager compares every upload with videos and music owned by others. If it finds a match, it can block the video, mute it, send your earnings to the owner, or give your page a strike. The best protection is simple: make content with nothing to match.

✅ Check this before every upload

  • I wrote the script (AI help is fine, copying a video is not).
  • I own or license every picture and clip, and I noted the source.
  • My voice and music are allowed for commercial use.
  • Any outside clip is short (under 20% of the video) and I add something new.
  • No watermark from TikTok or other apps.
  • AI label is on where the video looks real.

"Fair use" is a narrow US law decided case by case. It is not a free pass. If unsure, don't use the clip.

Chapter 5 · Original contentPage 13

Got a Warning? Do This.

Stay calm. Most warnings can be fixed.

  1. Read the notice. Go to Professional Dashboard → Monetization Status. It shows the post and the rule.
  2. Find the type. Copyright claim? Unoriginal content? Community rule?
  3. If you made it or have a license: appeal and attach proof (project files, license receipt, AI history).
  4. If you don't have rights: delete it, plus any other posts made the same way.
  5. Reset: post only clearly original content for 7–14 days before trying that style again.
Type of problemWhat to do
©️ Copyright claimLicensed? Dispute with proof. Not licensed? Remove the post.
🔁 Unoriginal contentAppeal if you made it; explain what you created.
📜 Community StandardsRead the exact rule before appealing.
🛡️ Protect your own videos: Once you have good originals, apply for Meta's content protection tool in your dashboard. It finds copies of your Reels and lets you take action.

Get Paid in India

06

Set up payouts the day your invite arrives. Facebook pays nothing until your bank and tax details are done.

🏦 Step 1: Create your payout account

  1. Go to Professional Dashboard → Monetization → Payouts.
  2. Choose Individual (unless you own a registered company).
  3. Type your name exactly as on your PAN and bank account. One wrong letter = failed payment.
  4. Add your home address in India.

💳 Step 2: Bank details

Use a bank that accepts foreign money

SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, Kotak and most big banks do. Account must be in your own name.

SWIFT vs IFSC

IFSC works only inside India. For money from abroad, banks use a SWIFT code. Ask your bank for the right one. Fill exactly what Facebook's form asks.

📅 Step 3: When does money come?

ItemDetail
Minimum payout$100 outside the US. Smaller amounts roll over to next month.
CurrencySent in US dollars. Your bank converts to rupees.
ProofKeep the bank's foreign remittance advice (FIRC) for every payment.

The US Tax Form (W-8BEN) Made Simple

Meta is a US company, so it needs this form. Without it, up to 30% can be cut from US-sourced income.

Form W-8BENExample of what to fill
FieldWhat you enter
NameYour name exactly as on PAN
Country of citizenshipIndia
Permanent addressYour home address in India (not a PO box)
US tax numberLeave blank
Foreign tax numberYour PAN
Date of birthAs on PAN
Tax treatyResident of India – follow the form's steps
SignSign online. You confirm you are not a US person.
Good to know: Many Indian creators who work fully from India see little or no US tax cut. But it depends on how Meta classifies each payment, so check every payout statement. The form is usually valid for 3 years.

🇮🇳 Taxes in India

📄 Income tax

Facebook income is taxable in India as business or professional income. Track income and costs, and file your ITR.

🧾 GST

Needed once yearly turnover crosses about ₹20 lakh. Foreign income is usually "export of services" – ask about an LUT.

📆 Advance tax

If your yearly tax is over ₹10,000, pay it in quarterly parts.

🗂️ Records

Keep payout statements, bank papers and your W-8BEN copy safely.

This is general information, not tax advice. Talk to a Chartered Accountant (CA).
